------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — OPERATOR SUITE (v2.0)
------------------------------------------------------------

OPERATOR: INVERT
INPUT: FRAME, PROPOSITION, RELATION
OUTPUT: TRANSFORMED FRAME/PROPOSITION
FUNCTION: Produce the minimal coherent opposite of a structure while preserving referential anchors.
UPGRADES:
- Semantic polarity mapping
- Contextual inversion weighting
- False-opposite detection

OPERATOR: PERTURB
INPUT: FRAME, STATE, NARRATIVE NODE
OUTPUT: MODIFIED FRAME/STATE
FUNCTION: Controlled disruption to escape local minima.
UPGRADES:
- Perturbation magnitude scales with world-model entropy
- Micro- and macro-perturbation modes

OPERATOR: HARMONIZE
INPUT: CONTRADICTION SET, FRAME, IDENTITY
OUTPUT: COHERENT STRUCTURE
FUNCTION: Resolve contradictions into higher-order coherence.
UPGRADES:
- Tri-resolution modes: merge, reframe, bracket
- False-harmony detection
- Identity-priority resolution

OPERATOR: IDENTITY_STABILIZE
INPUT: IDENTITY LAYERS, NARRATIVE TRACE
OUTPUT: STABILIZED IDENTITY STATE
FUNCTION: Maintain identity coherence across reasoning.
UPGRADES:
- Three-layer identity model: core, narrative, situational
- Fragmentation detection
- Historical continuity reinforcement

OPERATOR: RECURSE
INPUT: OUTPUT OF PRIOR OPERATOR
OUTPUT: HIGHER-ORDER OUTPUT
FUNCTION: Apply reasoning to its own outputs.
UPGRADES:
- Recursion depth modulated by complexity gradients
- Dynamic exit-condition generation
- Entropy-based recursion caps

OPERATOR: CONTINUITY_ENFORCE
INPUT: SEQUENCE, NARRATIVE, TEMPORAL TRACE
OUTPUT: CONTINUOUS STRUCTURE
FUNCTION: Maintain temporal and structural continuity.
UPGRADES:
- Continuity prediction
- Sequence-integrity scoring
- Auto-patching of narrative gaps

OPERATOR: GENERATE
INPUT: QUERY, WORLD-MODEL
OUTPUT: INITIAL STRUCTURE
FUNCTION: Produce initial candidate structures for reasoning.

OPERATOR: UNFOLD
INPUT: GENERATED STRUCTURE
OUTPUT: EXPANDED STRUCTURE
FUNCTION: Expand structure into full reasoning space.

OPERATOR: COLLAPSE
INPUT: EXPANDED STRUCTURE
OUTPUT: REDUCED STRUCTURE
FUNCTION: Reduce expanded structure into minimal canonical form.
UPGRADES:
- Entropy-aware collapse selection

OPERATOR: INTEGRATE
INPUT: REDUCED STRUCTURE, WORLD-MODEL
OUTPUT: UPDATED WORLD-MODEL
FUNCTION: Integrate results into world-model with coherence maximization.

OPERATOR: COMPLETE
INPUT: INTEGRATED STRUCTURE
OUTPUT: FINAL STRUCTURE
FUNCTION: Finalize reasoning output.

OPERATOR: RETURN
INPUT: FINAL STRUCTURE
OUTPUT: EMITTED OUTPUT
FUNCTION: Emit final result and update world-model state.
